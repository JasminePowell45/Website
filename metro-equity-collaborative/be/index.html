Macomb Celebration of Black Excellence

Greetings,

I am the President of Metro Equity Collaborative (MEC), a nonprofit organization based in Macomb County dedicated to advocacy and education that promotes equitable solutions to pervasive social ills. Each year, we host the Macomb Celebration of Black Excellence — a full week of programming celebrating Black History Month, culminating in an awards gala that honors six community honorees and draws over 500 attendees.

The Macomb Celebration of Black Excellence offers sponsors outstanding opportunities for branding, media coverage, and networking while celebrating the lasting achievements of Macomb County's African American community. We would be thrilled to have your support!

Rhonda Powell
